Privacy for the early-access list

This page covers only the details you give us on this early-access page.

What we collect

The details you type into the form: whether you are an agency, a landlord or a renter, your full name, your email address, your phone number if you add one, and your city. We also keep the time you signed up.

Why

To email you when PlotDesk opens in your city, and to contact you about early access. We don't use these details for anything else.

Who can see it

The PlotDesk team. The services we use to run this page and keep the list (our website host and a spreadsheet and email service) process it for us. We don't sell it or share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Until PlotDesk has opened and we've contacted the list, or until you ask us to remove you.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing us.
